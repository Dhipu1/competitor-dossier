Method, and the limits of it

Findings come from crawling {pages_crawled} pages across {site_count} sites on {date}, plus each site's own sitemap where one is published. Content gaps are measured, not estimated: every competitor passage is compared against the closest equivalent on {client}'s site, and the weakest matches are reported. Every cited URL was checked against the crawl before this document was produced.

What this audit does not include:

  • Search volumes, keyword rankings, and traffic estimates. These cannot be derived from a page and require a paid data provider. No figure in this report is a ranking or a traffic number.
  • Full site inventories for sites publishing no sitemap. Those are reported as unknown, which is not the same as small or inactive.
  • Pages behind logins, and anything a site's robots.txt asked us not to fetch.
