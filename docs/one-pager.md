# Competitor Content Intelligence

**Know exactly what your competitors publish that you don't — measured, not guessed.**

Most competitor research is someone clicking through rival websites for a day
and writing up impressions. This is a pipeline. It crawls your site and your
named competitors, compares every piece of their content against the closest
thing you have, and reports where the distance is largest — with a citation to
the exact page behind every claim.

## Why this beats a manual audit

| | Manual audit | This |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Whatever pages someone clicked | Every page crawled, systematically |
| Findings | Reviewer's impressions | Measured similarity, ranked by gap size |
| Sources | "I saw it somewhere on their blog" | Every claim links to its source page |
| Repeatable | Starts from scratch each time | Re-runs on a schedule, reports only changes |

---

## What's measured, and what isn't

Everything reported is counted from pages actually fetched: content depth,
meta description and heading coverage, internal linking, structured data, and
site scale and publishing cadence from each site's own sitemap.

**Not included:** search volumes, keyword rankings, and traffic estimates.
Those require a paid data subscription and are quoted separately if you want
them. No figure in any report is a ranking or a traffic number — if a report
can't measure something, it says so rather than estimating.
